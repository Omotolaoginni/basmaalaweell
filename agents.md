# 1. PROJECT DEFINITION AND OVERVIEW

## 1.1 What Is This Project?

This project involves designing and developing a sophisticated personal portfolio website for an executive leader working across social impact, philanthropy, inclusive economic growth, community development, and cross-sector partnerships.

The website is a digital representation of the client's professional identity, leadership experience, expertise, values, and contributions.

It is intended to establish a credible and distinctive online presence through which organisations, philanthropic institutions, nonprofit leaders, government representatives, private-sector partners, event organisers, and other professional stakeholders can discover her work, understand her professional value, and explore opportunities to engage with her.

Unlike a traditional CV, this website should not simply document employment history, qualifications, or professional milestones. It should communicate the bigger picture of who the client is, the work she leads, the systems and communities she works with, the value she brings to complex initiatives, and the impact she seeks to advance.

The website should function as a combination of:

- **Personal brand platform:** Establishing a recognisable and consistent professional identity.
- **Executive portfolio:** Presenting leadership experience, expertise, professional contributions, and selected achievements.
- **Professional credibility platform:** Demonstrating experience, thought leadership, and the substance behind her work.
- **Speaking and thought leadership platform:** Providing visibility for her public voice, speaking themes, and published ideas.
- **Professional engagement platform:** Creating clear pathways for organisations and individuals to connect, collaborate, and make enquiries.

The overall experience must communicate the client's ability to bring people, institutions, resources, and ideas together to advance meaningful social and economic change.

## 1.2 Project Purpose

The primary purpose of this website is to position the client as an experienced, credible, and thoughtful executive leader whose work advances inclusive growth and community-driven impact.

It should help visitors quickly understand:

1. Who she is and what she stands for.
2. What areas of work and leadership she specialises in.
3. The professional experience and perspective she brings.
4. How she approaches collaboration, leadership, and impact.
5. The communities and broader systems her work seeks to influence.
6. Where to explore her speaking, writing, and thought leadership.
7. How to contact her for professional opportunities and collaboration.

## 1.3 Intended Audience

The website is primarily designed for:

- Philanthropic organisations and foundations.
- Nonprofit and civil society leaders.
- Government institutions and public-sector stakeholders.
- Private-sector executives and corporate partners.
- International development organisations.
- Social impact leaders and practitioners.
- Event organisers and conference curators.
- Media, researchers, and thought leadership platforms.
- Organisations seeking experienced strategic, advisory, or partnership leadership.

The design and content should make sense to both people familiar with the social impact sector and visitors discovering the client's work for the first time.

## 1.4 Desired Visitor Experience

When someone visits the website, they should immediately recognise that they are engaging with an experienced executive leader with a clear professional identity and a meaningful body of work.

The experience should progress naturally:

- **Discover:** Understand who the client is through a compelling introduction.
- **Understand:** Explore her background, values, leadership philosophy, and areas of expertise.
- **Evaluate:** Discover her professional contributions, experience, and thought leadership.
- **Engage:** Explore speaking opportunities, collaboration, or other professional engagements.
- **Connect:** Find a straightforward way to contact her.

The website should communicate substance without overwhelming visitors with information.

## 1.5 Brand Positioning

The client's central brand headline is:

**Big Ideas, Real Impact.**

This statement represents the relationship between strategic thinking and practical outcomes. It reflects a professional identity built around developing ideas, connecting stakeholders, mobilising resources, strengthening institutions, and advancing initiatives that create meaningful change.

The brand should communicate:

- Strategic thinking.
- Collaborative leadership.
- Inclusive economic growth.
- Community-driven development.
- Cross-sector influence.
- Equity and representation.
- Purposeful execution.
- Measurable impact.

The website should demonstrate these qualities through its content, structure, visual language, and overall experience rather than relying on generic claims.

## 1.6 Project Scope

The website will contain six primary sections:

1. Home
2. About Me
3. Work With Me
4. Speaking
5. Writing
6. Contact Me

Each section has a distinct purpose within the overall professional narrative.

The website should be designed as one coherent digital experience, with consistent branding, navigation, typography, visual hierarchy, and responsive behaviour.
