    Senior consultant & strategy advisor

    Consultancy & Strategic Advisory

    Basma Alawee brings more than 15 years of experience to consultancy and strategic advisory across social impact, refugee inclusion, philanthropy, programme development, strategic partnerships and systems change.

    Her work connects nonprofits, government, philanthropy and the private sector to develop strategies, build partnerships, mobilise resources and advance inclusive economic growth — always with the communities affected at the centre. Her perspective is shaped by a professional background spanning engineering, finance and nonprofit leadership, alongside lived refugee experience.
