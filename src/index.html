About Me

Bridging Perspectives, Leading Change, Advancing Impact,

With a background in engineering, finance, and nonprofit leadership, alongside lived refugee experience, I bring a systems-oriented and community-centred perspective to executive leadership. I am an executive leader in social impact, philanthropy, and cross-sector partnerships, with more than 15 years of experience advancing inclusive economic growth and community-driven change.

Throughout my career, I have led national initiatives, managed multi-million-dollar programmes, mobilised significant financial and volunteer resources, and guided organisations through growth and transition. My expertise spans executive strategy and operations, fundraising and grantmaking, organisational development, coalition building, and cross-sector partnerships. I work with senior leaders across nonprofits, government, philanthropy, and the private sector to align institutional priorities with meaningful social impact.

Beyond organisational leadership, I contribute to advisory and governance initiatives that shape policy, philanthropy, and community leadership at national and international levels. Through public speaking and thought leadership, I champion representation, inclusive decision-making, and the leadership of communities with lived experience of displacement.

My work is driven by a commitment to creating systems and opportunities that enable refugees and underserved communities to lead, contribute, and thrive.
