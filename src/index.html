About Me

A leader shaped by lived experience.

Basma’s professional journey spans engineering, finance, and nonprofit leadership — a breadth of experience she brings to every initiative she leads.

For over 15 years she has advanced inclusive economic growth and community-driven change, leading and supporting national initiatives, managing multi-million-dollar programmes, and mobilising financial and volunteer resources for immigrant and refugee communities.

Her perspective is shaped by both her leadership experience and her lived refugee experience. It informs a deep commitment to equity, representation, and collaboration — and to ensuring that communities are active participants in the decisions that affect them.

Her broader commitment is to create opportunities for communities, particularly displaced people, to lead, contribute, and thrive.

Leadership values

  • Equity and representation
  • Collaboration across sectors
  • Community-led outcomes
  • Inclusive decision-making
  • Purposeful execution
